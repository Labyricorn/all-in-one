Turn a song into a timing map

This studio listens to your song and works out where the beats, sections and sung words fall. You then check the result against the audio and fix anything that is off. Everything stays on this computer.

  1. Choose a song. Pick an audio or video file and tell the studio whether it has lyrics.
  2. Wait for the analysis. It runs in the background, so you can close this page and come back.
  3. Review and fix. Play the song, watch the lyrics follow along, and correct any word that is early or late.
  4. Export. Download the corrected timing file for your video or animation.

      How the studio works

      The four steps

      Choose a song, wait for the analysis, review and fix the timing, then export. The bar at the top shows where you are; you can move between steps at any time.

      Reviewing

      The song and its waveform stay at the top while you work. Lyrics scroll along as the song plays. Turn off Follow playback, or just scroll yourself, to look around. Click a word to select it, then change its text or timing on the right.

      Words with a dashed red underline have no time. Amber words are guesses. Dotted grey words were timed with low confidence. The Needs attention tab lists them and can guess times for untimed words.
